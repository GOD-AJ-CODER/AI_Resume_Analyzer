# DESIGN.md — UI/UX Design System, Dual-Theme Tokens & Component Blueprint

## 1. Aesthetic Direction & Product Vibe
* **Design Archetype:** High-precision engineering workspace inspired by **Linear**, **Vercel Dashboard**, and **Raycast**.
* **Core UX Laws:**
  1. **Zero Decorative Bloat:** Every card, badge, and gauge must communicate a deterministic check, a quantified score, an actionable rewrite, or a verified job signal. Never use generic stock illustrations.
  2. **3-Second Information Hierarchy:** Within 3 seconds of scan completion, the user must immediately perceive:
     * Their **ATS Compatibility Score** (`0–100`)
     * Their **AI-Generation Probability** (`0–100%`)
     * Their **Top Actionable Bullet Fixes** and **Verified Fresh Job Matches**
  3. **First-Class Dual Theme (Light & Dark Mode):** The entire UI must look equally crisp in **Light Mode** (high-contrast daylight editorial look) and **Dark Mode** (deep carbon-zinc developer workspace). Controlled via a persistent toggle in the top navigation bar using `next-themes` (`attribute="class"`, `defaultTheme="dark"`, `enableSystem`).
  4. **Zero-Latency Perceived Performance:** Deterministic checks render at `0ms` while Groq Llama-3.3 semantic critiques and 3-Layer Job Credibility cards stream in with staged skeleton states.

  2. Dual-Theme Color System & Tailwind Token MappingEvery component in components/ and app/ MUST pair Light Mode base classes with explicit dark: variant classes.
